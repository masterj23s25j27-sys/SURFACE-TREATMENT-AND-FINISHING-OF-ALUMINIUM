# Aluminum cathodes

The supplied Aluminum Anodizers Council bulletin describes aluminum cathodes for sulfuric-acid anodizing and lists 6063 extrusion in T5/T6 temper and 1100 or 1350 sheet among commonly used options. It recommends 5356 filler for aluminum cathode/header welds and placing welds above the electrolyte. Verify the applicable process specification before changing an existing line.

**Strengths:** high conductivity, lower assembly weight than lead, and potential reduction in cell voltage and bath heat.

**Checks:** alloy/temper, acid exposure during idle periods, joint integrity, active area, current distribution, and aluminum buildup in the bath.

**Reference:** [AAC Technical Bulletin #1-00](https://cdn.ymaws.com/anodizing.site-ym.com/resource/resmgr/Docs/Technical_Bulletin_1-00_Sulf.pdf).
