# Cathode material alternatives

| Material | Typical position in this guide | Main considerations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Aluminum](aluminum/README.md) | Preferred baseline for many sulfuric-acid lines when the process specification allows it. | High conductivity; verify grade, temper, weld procedure, bath buildup, and electrical joints. |
| [Lead](lead/README.md) | Legacy option or retained where a controlled specification requires it. | Lower conductivity, greater weight, worker exposure, and lead-bearing wastewater/sludge. |
| [Stainless steel](stainless-steel/README.md) | Process-specific alternative. | Exact bath compatibility, corrosion, contamination, and approval must be demonstrated. |
| [Graphite](graphite/README.md) | Uncommon specialty alternative. | Brittle, uncommon, and requires evaluation of handling, particle shedding, and electrical contacts. |

The process specification and bath chemistry control the final selection. Material comparisons are not interchangeable between sulfuric, chromic, phosphoric, and other anodizing processes.
