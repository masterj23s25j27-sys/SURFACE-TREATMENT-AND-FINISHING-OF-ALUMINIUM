# Lead cathodes

Lead is a legacy cathode material and may be required by a specific approved process specification. It is substantially less conductive and heavier than aluminum. Lead handling, exposure controls, contaminated rinse streams, sludge classification, and disposal requirements must be included in the decision.

Do not replace a lead system with another material until the process owner and customer specification approve the change. The AAC bulletin compares energy and operating considerations for aluminum cathodes.
