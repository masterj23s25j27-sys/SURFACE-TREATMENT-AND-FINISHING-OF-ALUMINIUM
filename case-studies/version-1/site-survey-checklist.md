# Version 1 site survey checklist

Record measured data and evidence. Do not estimate dimensions from the supplied photo. Attach drawings, certificates, photographs, lab reports, and calibration records.

## Process and production

- [ ] Confirm tank process and bath name: ______________________________
- [ ] Chemical composition/additives and concentration range: ______________________________
- [ ] Normal / maximum / upset operating temperature: ______________________________
- [ ] Liquid density or specific gravity: ______________________________
- [ ] Product alloys and largest part/rack dimensions: ______________________________
- [ ] Active anodic area per representative load: ______________________________
- [ ] Target coating thickness, finish, tests, customer specification: ______________________________
- [ ] Loads per shift, shift pattern, cycle time, annual operating hours: ______________________________
- [ ] Current quality losses, defects, rework, and downtime: ______________________________

## Tank, lining, structure, and containment

- [ ] Internal tank dimensions and maximum/normal liquid level recorded.
- [ ] Construction material/grade and liner system verified from records or competent inspection.
- [ ] Tank wall, floor, ribs, welds, frame, anchors, and foundation documented.
- [ ] Bowing/deflection readings taken at repeatable marked locations.
- [ ] Nozzles, penetrations, repairs, seams, and visible damage photographed.
- [ ] Secondary containment volume, condition, leak detection, and collection route reviewed.
- [ ] Safe access, guarding, lifting points, and maintenance clearance reviewed.
- [ ] Approved test/inspection method selected by tank designer; no unapproved pressure test.

## Electrical and cathode/rack

- [ ] Rectifier make/model, topology, nameplate, rated voltage/current, age, and service history recorded.
- [ ] Actual voltage/current profile captured for light, normal, and maximum load.
- [ ] Meter calibration/status and current measurement method recorded.
- [ ] Bus/header/rack joint voltage drop and temperature measured by qualified staff.
- [ ] Polarity and identity of every far-end/sidewall vertical feature traced safely.
- [ ] Cathode material, exposed area, position, distance, and maintenance status confirmed.
- [ ] Rack surface area, contact number/pressure, arrangement, and hoist envelope recorded.

## Cooling and agitation

- [ ] Cooling equipment type, rated duty, flow, approach temperatures, and service status recorded.
- [ ] Bath temperatures mapped at multiple points with a representative load.
- [ ] Agitation type and flow/distribution measured across the bath.
- [ ] Pump, eductor, blower, diffuser, piping, and filter condition documented.
- [ ] Heat load checked against maximum actual current/voltage and ambient conditions.

## Air, water, and waste

- [ ] Exhaust hood and capture coverage mapped around the full operating opening.
- [ ] LEV airflow/static pressure/indicator and examination records reviewed.
- [ ] Rinse flow and water use metered by process stage.
- [ ] Drag-out/drip return practices recorded.
- [ ] Wastewater streams sampled and segregated by source and chemistry.
- [ ] ETP performance, sludge generation/disposal, permits, and monitoring records reviewed.
- [ ] Energy, water, chemicals, effluent, and sludge normalized per m² or kg treated.

## Evidence and approvals

- [ ] Existing drawings, tank certificates, material certificates, welding records, and repairs collected.
- [ ] Calibration and maintenance records collected.
- [ ] Baseline product test results and representative accepted/rejected samples preserved.
- [ ] Required engineering disciplines and local permit authorities identified.
