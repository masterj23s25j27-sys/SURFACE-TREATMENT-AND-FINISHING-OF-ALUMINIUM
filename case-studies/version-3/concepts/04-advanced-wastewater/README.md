# Concept 4 — advanced residual wastewater treatment

## Baseline train

Segregate and equalize compatible streams; measure flow and chemistry; use bench/jar tests to determine pH/reagent and coagulation needs; precipitate metals; settle/clarify or use suitable DAF/lamella separation; filter residual solids; dewater sludge; verify final parameters against the site's permitted limits. Track the licensed sludge outlet and reject streams.

## Add-on technologies to pilot selectively

- **Electrocoagulation:** a 2025 study evaluated mixed anodizing effluent and showed high removal for selected parameters in its matrix. It requires a local pilot and comparison with reagent precipitation.
- **Electrooxidation:** consider only for validated oxidizable residuals. In that same study, its tested condition did not meet the sewer standard for sulfate; it is not a standalone complete treatment recommendation.
- **UF/NF/RO:** may polish/recover water from appropriate pretreated streams. Characterize concentrate and demonstrate reuse quality.
- **Ion exchange/electrodialysis:** useful only for a selected ion/acid stream with regeneration or concentrate management resolved.
- **Sludge resource recovery:** assess composition, contaminants, specification and lawful market route. Dewatering alone does not create a usable product.

## Monitoring and control

Trend flow, pH, conductivity, tank levels and treatment alarms; maintain representative laboratory sampling for metals, sulfate, COD/TOC, solids and other permit analytes as applicable. Online instruments are control aids, not substitutes for permit-mandated tests.

## Engineering caution

Test shock loads, peak flow, mixed chemical compatibility, power consumption, electrode/membrane life, sludge amount and outage modes. No universal pH, current density, retention time or chemical dosage is specified here. See the [method matrix](../../METHOD-COMPARISON.md).
