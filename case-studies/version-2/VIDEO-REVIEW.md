## How to complete the record

For every actual observation, add the exact timestamp and describe only what is visible or audible. Use a separate **interpretation** column for possible function and a **verification needed** column for polarity, material, process, dimensions or hidden connections. Capture still frames locally at scene changes and include them only if they are legible and are the owner's media or otherwise permitted for repository publication. Do not infer missing sequences from elapsed time alone.

Suggested entry format:

| Timestamp | Direct observation | Possible interpretation (unverified) | Verification needed | Frame file |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `mm:ss` | `Describe visible facts` | `Possible function and confidence` | `Plant record/test/site check` | `frames/clip-mm-ss.png` |

Once reviewed, revise `REPORT.md` to separate clip evidence from the separate Version 1 photograph and general engineering recommendations. Preserve this source record and note who reviewed it and on what date.
