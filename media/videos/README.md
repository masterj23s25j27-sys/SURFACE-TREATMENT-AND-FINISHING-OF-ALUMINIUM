# Video and media references

This repository contains original SVG schematics. The links below include manufacturer or equipment-provider demonstrations of thermoplastic tank fabrication and welding, plus optional anodizing context. They are external references only; they do not replace a qualified weld procedure, a tank-fabricator drawing, or a site-specific engineering review.

| Resource | Relevance | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Munsch extrusion welder demonstration for PP chemical tanks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Hhs0x_uZi0g) | Equipment-provider demonstration of extrusion welding PP sheet for plastic chemical tanks. | Shows a welding tool/process, not a complete engineering design or weld qualification. |
| [Munsch mini extruder welding PP/PE sheet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h11GwK-9Jww) | Demonstrates extrusion welding used in thermoplastic tank fabrication. | Tool demonstration; parameters and joint acceptance must come from a qualified procedure. |
| [Valk Welding plastic tank extrusion robot](https://vimeo.com/527858800) | Shows automated extrusion welding of plastic tank products. | Automated system example; not a construction specification for this tank. |
| [DIY Aluminum Sulfuric Acid Anodizing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XamApx4sAqo) | General process explanation; includes a short segment on cathode positioning. | Small-scale demonstration, not an industrial tank support design. |
| [Anodize Your Own Aluminum Parts](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jCZILZHbCIc) | Demonstrates basic anode/cathode roles in a small setup. | Hobby scale; not construction guidance for production equipment. |

Video links are external and may change. No third-party footage is copied into this repository. Contributions should add only relevant, lawful links and state the scale and limitations clearly.
