# Video and media references

This repository contains original SVG schematics because no authoritative industrial video demonstrating construction of a PP-tank cathode support assembly was identified during preparation. External videos below are optional background only; they do not replace the AAC bulletin, a tank-fabricator drawing, or a site-specific engineering review.

| Resource | Relevance | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [DIY Aluminum Sulfuric Acid Anodizing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XamApx4sAqo) | General process explanation; includes a short segment on cathode positioning. | Small-scale demonstration, not an industrial tank support design. |
| [Anodize Your Own Aluminum Parts](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jCZILZHbCIc) | Demonstrates basic anode/cathode roles in a small setup. | Hobby scale; not construction guidance for production equipment. |

Video links are external and may change. No third-party footage is copied into this repository. Contributions should add only relevant, lawful links and state the scale and limitations clearly.
